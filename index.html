    Gestures

      Puppet: your fingertips hold the robot on strings; move or turn your hand and it follows at once. Take the hand away and the strings fall.

      Walk / Run: raise index + middle (V) to walk; add the ring finger (three fingers) to run. Move the hand left / right to steer.

      Squat: touch the thumb and index tips together and let go quickly: one squat per tap.

      Heavy lifting: pinch and hold, then raise the hand to lift the robot, move sideways to turn it.

      Spin: index finger straight up, circle it like a tornado; the robot spins as fast as you circle.

      Face: the robot looks at you; it smiles when you smile, is sad when you are, and turns / raises its head like you (mirror).

      Laugh / Sad: thumb up / thumb down, hold still.

      Stop: stop the motion, hand out of view, L (lock), Esc (camera off). R returns home.

      Keys: L lock · W wave · R reset · H HUD · Esc stop
